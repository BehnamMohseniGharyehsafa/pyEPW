# Community conduct

Use respectful, constructive language. Discuss evidence and implementation
choices without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination. Welcome
questions from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Credit contributions and data providers. Do not publish someone's private
information, credentials, precise private location or correspondence without
permission. Scientific disagreements should preserve methods, sources and
uncertainty so others can reproduce the result.

The maintainer, [Behnam Mohseni Gharyehsafa](https://github.com/BehnamMohseniGharyehsafa),
may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Request a private
reporting channel through a minimal issue when needed; do not post confidential
details in public. Decisions should be proportionate and focus on restoring a
safe, productive project discussion.
